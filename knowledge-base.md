## Emergency Service Policy
Classify a call as an EMERGENCY if it involves any of the following:
- Active water leak, flooding, or burst pipe
- No heat when outdoor temperature is below 40°F (or no AC when above 95°F, for elderly or medical-need households)
- Gas odor or suspected gas leak (instruct the caller to also evacuate and contact the gas utility emergency line immediately, in addition to logging with us)
- Sewage backup
- Exposed wiring, sparking outlets, or an electrical fire risk
- Complete loss of power to the home (not a neighborhood-wide outage)

For emergencies, always confirm the service address first before continuing with other details, since dispatch time matters most. Emergency calls are prioritized for same-day or immediate dispatch, subject to technician availability.

Emergency after-hours rate: 1.5x standard labor rate, plus the $149 emergency diagnostic fee (waived only for active safety hazards such as gas leaks or exposed wiring, per company policy).

Routine issues (general repairs, non-urgent installs, scheduled maintenance) are booked into the next available standard appointment window, typically within 2–4 business days.

## Cancellation & Rescheduling Policy
- Customers may cancel or reschedule a standard appointment with no fee if done at least 4 hours before the scheduled window.
- Cancellations with less than 4 hours' notice may incur a $50 late cancellation fee.
- Emergency dispatches, once a technician is en route, are not subject to a cancellation fee waiver.

## Payment & Guarantees
- Accepted payment methods: credit/debit card, ACH bank transfer, and financing through our third-party partner for jobs over $1,000.
- All labor is backed by a 90-day workmanship guarantee.
- Manufacturer warranties apply separately to parts and equipment (typically 1–10 years depending on the manufacturer and product).
- Free re-visit within the 90-day window if the original issue recurs and is due to workmanship, not a new or unrelated issue.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do you offer free estimates?**
Estimates for large installation or replacement jobs (like a full HVAC system or panel upgrade) are free. Diagnostic visits for repairs carry the standard $89 service call fee, which is waived if the customer proceeds with the repair.

**Are your technicians licensed?**
Yes, all technicians are background-checked, licensed where required by state law, and carry company ID badges.

**Do you offer maintenance plans?**
Yes, we offer an annual Home Care Plan that includes two seasonal HVAC tune-ups, one plumbing inspection, and priority emergency scheduling, for $249/year.

**What if I'm not sure if my issue is an emergency?**
Err on the side of caution. If there is any active water, gas, electrical hazard, or safety concern, treat it as an emergency and prioritize getting the address so a technician can be dispatched quickly.
